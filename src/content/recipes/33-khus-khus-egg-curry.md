  - "Separately, fry each in a dry pan without oil: the Khus Khus, Jeera, Cinnamon, Cloves .  Grind in a coffee grider and remove to a plate."
  - Fry the Coconut shavings and Chillis in the oil until golden brown and remove them to the same plate..
  - Place the Dry Masala from the plate   in a blender and grind them.  Add Water if needed to get a fine paste.
  - Fry the Tomatoes, Onions, Garlic, and Ginger in the same pan gently for about 10 minutes until limp..
  - Add this Wet Masala to the Dry Masala in the blender and process.
