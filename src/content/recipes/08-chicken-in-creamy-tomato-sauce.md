steps:
  - Heat oil
  - Add Cardamom, Cloves and Cinnamon and fry for 30 seconds
  - Add onions and saute until onions are light brown, about 15 minutes
  - Add ginger and saute for about 4 minutes
  - Add Coriander, Cumin and Turmeric and saute for 30 seconds
  - Add tomatoes and saute until the oil seperates from the gravy, about 10 minutes
  - Turn off the heat, add one cup of water and mix
  - Blend the curry for 5 minutes in electric blender and let cool
  - Put chicken in a big pot, add the salt and rub the salt on it
  - Pour the pureed sauce on it and let sit for 1 to 2 hrs
  - Put the pot on the burner, bring to a boil while stirring constantly
  - Turn down the heat to simmer and let the chicken cook for about 20 minutes
  - Add half and half and mix
  - Add sugar and mix
  - Adjust the seasonings and serve
