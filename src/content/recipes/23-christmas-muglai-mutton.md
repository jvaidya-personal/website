steps:
  - Prepare all ingredients first, including spices on three different small plates.
  - Fry the onions in 1/2 or more of the oil until they are translucent.
  - Add Plate 1,  with the wet spices, and continue frying until the onions are fragrant and brownish but not crisp.
  - Remove all of the cooked ingredients with a spatula and set them aside, but leave as much oil as possible in the pan.
  - Add Plate 2, with the whole spices,  and fry them on medium for a few minutes.
  - Turn the heat as high as possible.   Add oil if needed.  When hot, add the mutton in two or three batches and brown it well.
  - As each piece is browned, put it aside, and add more raw meat.
  - Return all of the mutton to the pan, add the Ghee, and heat it all on medium, then add Plate 3, with the powdered spices, and fry lightly.
  - Add the Onion mixture back in, and heat everything up again.
  - Add Sour Cream and Yoghurt.   Heat to simmer.
  - Cover and cook gently for 45 minutes.
  - Taste and add more Salt and Powdered Chillies, if needed.
