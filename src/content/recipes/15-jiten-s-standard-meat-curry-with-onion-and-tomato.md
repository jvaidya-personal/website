steps:
  - Fry the onions gently in the oil until they are translucent, approximately 20 minutes.
  - Add the Garlic and Ginger.  Fry gently for 5 minutes more.
  - Add the whole spices and fry for 15 more minutes.  The onions shoulc appear lightly golden.
  - Add the powdered spices and fry them for two minutes.
  - Turn up the stove to high, and add the Tomatoes.  Cook until the tomatoes are hot.
  - Add the Chicken,  get it it hot also, then fry it for 6 minutes.
  - Heat the water to boiling, separately, then add it to the Curry.
  - Bring to a boil, reduce the heat, and simmer very gently for an additional 20 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked.
