steps:
  - Fry the Ginger and Chillies in the Oil.
  - Add the Onions and fry gently until translucent.
  - Add the Carrots, then sprinkle with Turmeric, then fry until eveything is hot and the turmeric cooks a bit.
  - Add the Chicken and the Spices, but only 1/2 of the Garam Masala and fry.  Stir and scrape the pot  vigorously until the chicken has lost its pinkiness.
  - Add the Coconut Milk,  the Sugar, and the rest of the Garam Masala, and the Spinach if you are making the &quot;Palak&quot; version.
  - Heat, covered, on a low frame for 20 minutes.  Simmer gently.  Stir and scrape the pot occasionally.
  - Serve in small bowls topped with chopped Cilantro and a few drops of Lemon.
