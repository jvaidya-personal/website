steps:
  - "These are rich-tasting but healthy, particularly for special diets.  They are: all-vegetarian,  gluten-free, wheat-free, and dairy-free.  They are baked, so they are easier to make than the usual deep-fried koftas, and unbelievably, they taste better."
  - "Cook potatoes: bring water to boil with 1 tsp salt, add potatoes, boil for 30 minutes or more until soft.  Keep the water for the next steps."
  - "Cook the cauliflower: wash, cut into large florets, boil water and add florets, boil for 15 minutes or until soft"
  - "Cook peas: boil water, add peas, bring to a second boil, lower heat cook covered for 8 to 10 minutes"
  - Remove vegetables from water as soon as they are cooked.
  - Peel the boiled potatoes.
  - Powder the nuts coarsely using the smaller container of the food processor.
  - Grate the raw Carrots using the larger container of the food processor.
  - Put potatoes, cauliflower and peas in a large vessel and mash by hand with a potato masher
  - Mix grated carrots in with the vegetables using the masher
  - Add Ginger, Chillies, Cumin, Salt and Cilantro to the mix and continue to mash
  - Add the nuts to the vegetable mash and mix in thoroughly using the masher
  - Add Besan (Garbanzo flour) and mix in thoroughly with the masher
  - Preheat oven to 375 F.
  - Oil two baking sheets.
  - Oil your hands,  using about 3 T of mash for each form the vegetable mash into Koftas and place them on the baking sheet. Makes approx 48 Koftas.
  - Bake the Koftas for 45 minutes
  - Take the baking sheets out and flip the Koftas
  - Bake the Koftas again for 45 minutes
  - These may be frozen until needed.  They may be reheated in VeganTomato Nut Cream Sauce for a sumptious vegan, gluten free dish, or reheated and served with catsup.
