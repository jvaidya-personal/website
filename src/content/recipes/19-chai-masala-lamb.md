steps:
  - I like this recipe because of the novelty of using chai masala for something other than chai.  Anyway, chai masala is perfect for lamb.
  - The ingredients are given per pound of meat.  The recipe is best with 2-6 lbs.
  - Put all ingredients in a slow cooker or stove, on low heat, covered. It should simmer only slightly, or not at all.
  - Mix well.  Cook for 6 hours or until done
  - This makes a lightly seasoned meat and broth that you can eat plain, or add other ingredients such as cooked pasta, rice, or garbanzos; raw vegetables such as potatoes, greens, peas, carrots, cilantro, and green beans.
  - Add more chai masala and salt to taste if necessary, particularly if you add pasta or potatoes.
