steps:
  - Makes 2-8-inch round pans plus 12 or less cupcakes
  - "Materials:  Kitchenaid or other stand mixer"
  - Preheat oven to 375.
  - Put the butter and sugar into the mixer bowl with the paddle, allow to come to room temperature.
  - Sift the dry ingredients together with a sifter onto 2 dinner plates. 3 X
  - Put the Eggs and the Extracts together in a large bowl, and mix with a fork until uniform color.
  - Line two round pans on the bottom only with parchment paper and oil them
  - Put liners into the cupcake tins, or oil them. Alternatively you can use a loaf pan for excess batter.
  - Cream the Butter and Sugar together on 6 or 8 until it is a little fluffy.
  - Add Egg yolks, Extracts, and beat on 6 or 8 until uniform.
  - Alternately add the Dry ingredients and the milk while mixing.
  - Beat at 6 or 8 for some time until the mixture gets puffy.
  - Transfer the batter to the pans, filling them about 2/3 or less full.  A ladle is easiest.  Roll the pan in circles to moisten the sides, and also use the ladle to make the batter more on the sides and less in the middle.
  - "Bake them until a toothpick can be removed cleanly. "
  - Remove pans from oven and let them cool to room temperature.
  - Frost with butter-cream frosting.  Orange flavored is good.  The middle can be either frosting or strawberry jam.ere
