steps:
  - Clean and cut the vegetables and put them on a big plate
  - Measure out the spices into separate heaps on a small plate.  (Each heap will be put in separately by brushing it from the plate with a dry thumb.)
  - If making the Cabbage salad, add the salt 15 minutes or as much as 2 hours  before serving.)
  - For other salads, assemble the ingredients 1/2 hour or less before eating.
  - Mix together the Ingredients for the particular salad.
  - To make the Phodni, heat the oil in a small pan.  Add the whole chillies and Mustard Seed (Mohri).  Fry until half or more of the Mohri turns greyish and/or pops.  Remove the pan from the flame.  Add Turmeric and swirl.  Add the Hing (Asafetida), swirl briefly and pour it into the salad.
  - Pour Phodni over the salad and mix again
  - Taste and adjust seasonings.  If the salad is not spicy enough, add Mirchi (Caynne or ground red chillies).
  - For best results, use organic ingredients.  The flavors are much better.
  - (Carrot and Cabbage can be grated in the food processor ahead of time, for several meals.  Grated cabbage and carrots will keep in a plastic bag in the refrigerator for up to a week.)
