आज श्रीनिकेत देशपांडे याची पोस्ट वाचून दोन-चार वर्षांपूर्वी एका मित्राला लिहिलेल्या या इमेलची आठवण झाली.

"I was thinking a little more about the resentment of the English-speaking elite or elite in general that we were discussing last night. I said to the young man from Odisha that this was part of the human condition. But I wanted to say a little more, to contextualize what I meant by that.

Basically, privilege is a continuum. You look at people that you perceive to be higher in privilege than you and it generates certain feelings of exclusion in you and you impute some intentionality to the people that you perceive to be more privileged than you, and this leads to feelings of resentment.

What your fail to realize is that right below you in the privilege continuum, there is a  class of people who are probably feeling the same resentment towards you. Once you make this leap, you realize that  you have no ill will towards people in this class below you, mostly you don't think about them too much. You might even be willing to help them if the need arises.

This allows you to more realistically model what people you perceive higher than you on the privilege continuum might think of you. This combination of more realistic model of what people above you might think of you and empathy for people below you makes the resentment evaporate.

Does this make sense?"

आणि हे असंच सर्व काळात जगात सर्व ठिकाणी लोकांना वाटलेलं आहे आणि वाटतच असतं. Czeslaw Milosz या पोलीश कवीची Youth ही सुंदर कविता याच विषयावर आहे.  कमेंट मध्ये देत आहे. पोस्टबरोबर त्याचाच फोटो टाकत आहे कारण मला त्याचा हा या माणसाला आता कुठलेच भ्रम उरलेले नाहीत असे जाणवून देणारा, धारदार नजरेचा, अस्सल स्लाविक फोटो फार आवडतो.
